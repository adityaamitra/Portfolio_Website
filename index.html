About
About

I'm a software engineer in Boston with 3 years at Tata Consultancy Services and a Master's in Information Systems from Northeastern University. I build full-stack web applications and RESTful APIs with FastAPI, Flask, React, and TypeScript, backed by SQL and NoSQL databases — with the dashboards, logging, and automation that keep them reliable.

At TCS I worked on core banking production systems for a premier financial institution: I was promoted to Systems Engineer, led a team of three rolling out an ELK Stack pipeline, and automated roughly 60% of manual infrastructure work with Python and shell scripts. That environment taught me the habits I bring to everything I build — root-cause fixes over symptom patches, and production reliability as a feature, not an afterthought.

More recently I've been building AI systems end-to-end — RAG pipelines, multi-agent orchestration, LLM fine-tuning, and deep reinforcement learning — and shipping them as real, deployed applications rather than notebooks.

Away from the keyboard, I play varsity-level table tennis and won an NCTTA divisional championship representing Northeastern.
